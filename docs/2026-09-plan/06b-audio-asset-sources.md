# Audio asset sources

Researched but **not auditioned**: listen to previews before committing. Prices are as listed at time of research; unlisted prices are unknown. Check each license before shipping (CC BY requires attribution).

## Engine loops

| Asset | Link | Notes |
|-------|------|-------|
| Rally Engine Sounds Vol. 1 (Skril Studio) | https://skrilstudio.com/portfolios/rally-engine-sounds-vol-1/ | Best fit. 3 rally cars; each 40 seamless-looping stereo WAVs (20 **interior**, 20 exterior) incl. on-/off-throttle variants across idle→max RPM. Unity version bundles turbo, straight-cut gearbox, and muffler-crackle clips (usable for heat cues). Add-on to Realistic Engine Sounds 2 but ships raw WAVs. Sold on Unity Asset Store, FAB, GameDev Market. Price unknown. |
| IndieSFX S017C Car Sounds | https://indiesfx.itch.io/indiesfx-s017c | 91 SFX, mono+stereo 44.1 kHz WAV. Interior/exterior car engine loops, racing engine lo/mid/hi (interior and exterior), skid-with-engine, heavy/medium crashes, bumps, gear changes. Loops designed for pitching. Price unknown. |
| Racing Sound Pack (Cascadia Games) | https://cascadiagames.itch.io/racing-sound-pack | $5+. 94 OGG files, 5 vehicles; car set has engine loops at 4 speeds, plus crashes, screeches, spin-outs. No resale or repackaging. Cheapest complete start. |
| Car Engines (Magic Sound Effects) | https://magicsoundeffects.itch.io/car-engines | 14 vehicles (8 city cars, 4 sports cars, 1 diesel, 1 off-road); idle/low/mid/high/very-high loops (70 seamless), accel/decel and transition clips. License: personal use + one commercial project. Price unknown. |
| Rally Cars Sound Effects (Debsound) | https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/audio/sound-fx/transportation/rally-cars-sound-effects-134616 | $14.99, Unity Asset Store, 2018. Keywords suggest pass-bys; loop content unconfirmed. |
| Car Engine Loop (qubodup, Freesound) | https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/147242/ | Opel Astra 1.6 16V, 4 s stereo WAV, 96 kHz/24-bit. CC BY 4.0. Login required to download. |
| Car Sound Effects Pack, Low Quality (OpenGameArt) | https://opengameart.org/content/car-sound-effects-pack-low-quality | 13 CC0 sounds, phone-recorded, 64 kb/s. Placeholder only. |
| i6 German – Free Engine Sound Pack | https://assetstore.unity.com/packages/audio/sound-fx/transportation/i6-german-free-engine-sound-pack-106037 | Free, Unity Asset Store (Skril Studio). Straight-six; likely off-genre. |
